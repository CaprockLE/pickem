STEP 1Pool Backtest can the pool's picks be predicted from past weeks?

Nobody sees anyone's sheet before the deadline, so the optimizer predicts the pool from each player's past habits. This tests that: every finished week is predicted using only the other weeks, then compared with what the pool actually picked.

STEP 2This Week's Sheet the picks with your best chance to finish first

Simulates the week thousands of times — every game rolled from the betting odds, everyone likely to play filling in a sheet from their habits — then starts from all favourites and flips a game only when it raises your chance to win. Uses the backtest's best model (run automatically if needed).
